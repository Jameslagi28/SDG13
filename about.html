About Deforestation

Understanding the causes and consequences of forest loss in the Philippines

What is Deforestation?

Deforestation is the large-scale clearing of forests, often caused by farming, mining, logging, and urban growth. This process removes entire forest ecosystems, converting forested land to non-forest use.

Globally, deforestation fuels climate change by releasing stored carbon and reducing the Earth's capacity to absorb CO₂. It also leads to massive biodiversity loss as species lose their natural habitats.

Locally, deforestation endangers communities by removing natural barriers against floods, landslides, and storms, while destroying sources of food, medicine, and livelihoods.

The Philippines Reality

A century ago, lush forests covered most of the country. Today, less than one-fourth remains. From the Sierra Madre to Palawan, forests that protect us from floods and storms are under constant threat.
