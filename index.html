Why Forests Matter

Philippine forests are vital ecosystems that support life, communities, and our planet's climate

Ecosystem

Forests purify air and water, regulate climate, and prevent floods.

Biodiversity

The Philippines is home to more than 52,000 species of plants and animals; 67% of terrestrial species are endemic.

Communities

Over 20 million Filipinos depend on forests for food, shelter, and income.

Climate

Philippine forests store approximately 2.1 billion tons of carbon, buffering the effects of climate change.
