Solutions & Hope

Discover how government programs, NGO efforts, and individual actions are working to restore our forests

Government Initiatives

National Greening Program (NGP)

The Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR) implements the NGP, a major forest rehabilitation initiative aimed at restoring degraded forestlands for poverty alleviation, food security, biodiversity conservation, and climate change mitigation.

2023 Achievements:

  • ₱2.39 billion allocated for forest rehabilitation
  • 7+ million seedlings planted
  • 13,565 hectares covered
  • 158,843 hectares maintained

Military Partnership

The Philippine Army partners in reforestation efforts with campaigns like planting one million trees, including food-bearing trees to promote food security alongside environmental restoration.

Extended Goals:

  • Program extended until 2028
  • Focus on community stewardship
  • Sustainable tree growing approach
  • Food security integration

NGO & Civil Society Efforts

Haribon Foundation

Focuses on biodiversity conservation, resilience building, and bringing nature-based solutions to tackle climate challenges through community engagement.

WWF Philippines

Actively engages in natural resource management, wildlife conservation, and climate change advocacy through research and community programs.

Greenpeace Philippines

Conducts impactful campaigns and community initiatives for environmental protection, focusing on forest conservation and sustainable development.

Increased Climate Funding

The government has significantly increased its climate change budget, with a 60.1% rise in funding allocations from 2022 to 2023, demonstrating stronger commitment to environmental protection.

Target: Plant 5 million trees by 2028

Community Collaboration

Growing community engagement with stakeholders collaborating closely for reforestation projects and food security-oriented planting, emphasizing collective environmental protection.

Focus: Sustainable tree growing and protection
